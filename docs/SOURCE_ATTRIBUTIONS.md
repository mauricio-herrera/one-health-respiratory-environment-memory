# Source attributions

This integrated dataset combines variables derived from multiple public sources.
The original source data remain attributable to the organizations listed below.

## Ministerio de Salud / SADU

Source: Ministerio de Salud de Chile / Departamento de Estadísticas e
Información de Salud (DEIS), Atenciones de Urgencia.

Suggested acknowledgement:

> Urgent-care data used in this research were obtained from publicly available
> Chilean Ministry of Health / DEIS administrative data and were subsequently
> aggregated, harmonized, and processed by the authors.

## DEIS mortality and hospital-discharge data

Source: Departamento de Estadísticas e Información de Salud (DEIS), Ministerio
de Salud de Chile.

Suggested acknowledgement:

> Mortality and hospital-discharge information was derived from publicly
> available DEIS datasets. The integrated regional variables and derived
> features in this archive were produced by the authors.

## SINCA

Source: Sistema de Información Nacional de Calidad del Aire (SINCA), Ministerio
del Medio Ambiente de Chile.

The Ministry's official air-quality FAQ states that SINCA monitoring data are
available for direct download and that researchers may use historical and
real-time observations for studies of pollution, environmental health, climate
change, and related topics.

Suggested acknowledgement:

> Air-quality observations were obtained from the Sistema de Información
> Nacional de Calidad del Aire (SINCA), Ministerio del Medio Ambiente de Chile.
> Regional aggregates and derived variables were constructed by the authors for
> academic research and reproducibility.

Source information:
https://mma.gob.cl/contacto/preguntas-frecuentes/calidad-del-aire/

## ERA5 / Copernicus

Source: Copernicus Climate Change Service (C3S), ERA5.

Suggested acknowledgement:

> Contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information. ERA5-derived
> regional indicators and memory variables were produced by the authors.

## Instituto Nacional de Estadísticas (INE)

Source: Instituto Nacional de Estadísticas de Chile.

Suggested acknowledgement:

> Population information was derived from publicly available data of the
> Instituto Nacional de Estadísticas (INE), Chile.

## CASEN

Source: Ministerio de Desarrollo Social y Familia, Encuesta de Caracterización
Socioeconómica Nacional (CASEN).

Suggested acknowledgement:

> The vulnerability representation used in this release was derived by the
> authors from publicly available CASEN information. The derived index is not an
> official CASEN indicator.

## General statement

The inclusion of source-derived variables in this research archive does not
imply endorsement of the study, methods, models, or conclusions by any source
organization.
