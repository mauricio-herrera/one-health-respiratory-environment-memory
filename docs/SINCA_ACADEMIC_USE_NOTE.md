# SINCA academic-use note

The SINCA component of this release consists of regional research variables
derived from publicly accessible air-quality observations.

The Ministerio del Medio Ambiente states in its official air-quality FAQ that:

- SINCA contains air-quality and meteorological monitoring data from public and
  private monitoring stations;
- the data can be downloaded directly from the SINCA website; and
- researchers can use historical and real-time data for studies including air
  pollution, environmental health, and climate change.

This release therefore includes the SINCA-derived regional variables as part of
the complete integrated academic dataset.

The research team does not claim ownership over the original SINCA
observations. SINCA / Ministerio del Medio Ambiente is explicitly acknowledged
as the source. The contribution of this project is the regional integration,
quality control, harmonization, derived indicators, model inputs, and analysis.
