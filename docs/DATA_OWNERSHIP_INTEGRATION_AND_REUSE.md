# Data ownership, integration, and reuse statement

## What this release is

This archive is an **integrated research dataset** assembled from multiple
publicly accessible governmental and Copernicus data sources.

The research team does **not** claim ownership of the original SADU, DEIS, GRD,
SINCA, ERA5/Copernicus, INE, CASEN, or other source observations.

The original contribution of this release is the reproducible research layer
built on top of those sources, including:

- source integration;
- regional and temporal harmonization;
- cleaning and quality control;
- canonical key construction;
- documented aggregation;
- feature engineering;
- environmental-memory construction;
- health-memory construction;
- vulnerability integration;
- model design;
- numerical auditing;
- prospective prediction;
- external validation;
- uncertainty analysis; and
- reproducibility metadata.

## Redistribution policy

The complete integrated dataset is released for scientific transparency,
reproducibility, and academic reuse.

Source-derived components remain subject to the attribution and reuse conditions
of their respective original sources. This archive does not attempt to replace,
supersede, or broaden those source terms.

The research team imposes no additional restriction on the underlying public
data beyond the conditions attached to their original sources.

The original software/code authored for this project is separately covered by
the MIT licence included in the release.

## Citation

Researchers using this integrated dataset should cite:

1. the archived research release / associated scientific article; and
2. the original source organizations relevant to the variables they use.

See `SOURCE_ATTRIBUTIONS.md` and `SOURCE_RIGHTS_POLICY.csv`.
