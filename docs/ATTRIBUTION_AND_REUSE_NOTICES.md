# Attribution and reuse notices

This release candidate contains **derived, aggregated** variables from multiple
source systems. No blanket data licence is asserted for all files.

## SADU — Ministerio de Salud / Datos.gob.cl

The current Datos.gob.cl resource for *Atenciones de urgencias de causas
respiratorias por semana epidemiológica* is labelled **Creative Commons
Non-Commercial (Any)**.

Source:
https://datos.gob.cl/dataset/atenciones-de-urgencia-causas-respiratorias/resource/ae6c9887-106d-4e98-8875-40bf2b836041

The exact Creative Commons non-commercial variant/legal text must still be
confirmed before final deposition. This release must not grant broader rights
than the source permits.

## Copernicus / ERA5

Copernicus Products may be reproduced, distributed, adapted and combined under
the applicable Copernicus licence, subject to attribution.

For modified/adapted ERA5-derived material, include a notice equivalent to:

> Contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service Information [YEAR].

Also cite the applicable ERA5 Climate Data Store catalogue entry and include the
required no-endorsement/no-liability language from the Copernicus guidance.

## Instituto Nacional de Estadísticas (INE), Chile

INE states that its website content is governed by **Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)**. Reuse requires
appropriate attribution, a link to the licence, indication of changes, and
share-alike treatment where the licence applies to adaptations.

Source:
https://www.ine.gob.cl/terminos-de-uso-y-licencia-de-datos-abiertos

Because this release combines multiple source families, do not automatically
apply a single global licence to the entire data package.

## CASEN

CASEN documentation states that its databases are made publicly available and
may be used for research and publication, with accurate source attribution.

Source:
https://observatorio.ministeriodesarrollosocial.gob.cl/storage/docs/casen/2022/Manual_para_la_Investigacion_Casen_2022.pdf

The current audit does not infer a Creative Commons redistribution licence for
the derived vulnerability index. Preserve attribution and confirm derivative
redistribution terms before final publication.

## DEIS / GRD / SINCA

Exact source-specific licence evidence for the historical DEIS, GRD and SINCA
inputs represented in the canonical panel remains unresolved. Current catalogue
licences for other datasets from the same institutions must **not** be
automatically transferred to these exact source files.

Until exact rights are bound, these source-derived fields remain under manual
rights review.
